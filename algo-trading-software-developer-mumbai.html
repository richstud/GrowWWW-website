Frequently asked questions

Can you build a trading bot for my strategy in Mumbai?

Yes. Share your entry, exit and risk rules - even in plain words or as a TradingView indicator - and we write, backtest and deploy them on your broker's API.

Which brokers do you work with?

Zerodha Kite Connect, Upstox, Angel One SmartAPI, Dhan, Fyers and other brokers with an official API, plus Interactive Brokers and crypto exchanges.

Is algo trading legal for retail traders?

Yes, through your broker and within SEBI's framework for retail algo trading. We set bots up to follow your broker's rules for API access and strategy registration.

Do you give trading tips or guarantee returns?

No. We build and maintain the software; the strategy and decisions are yours, and trading always carries risk.
